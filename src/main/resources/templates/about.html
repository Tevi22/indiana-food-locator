About Indiana Food Locator

Connecting Indiana residents with food assistance resources in their communities.

Our Mission

Indiana Food Locator is designed to make it easier for individuals and families to find food assistance resources throughout Indiana.

The application provides a centralized way to search for food pantries, meal programs, mobile pantries, and other community resources by city or ZIP code.

What You Can Do

Search Resources

Search for food assistance locations by city, ZIP code, and food assistance type.

View Details

Review location addresses, hours, contact information, and requirements.

Save Favorites

Signed-in users can save food assistance locations for easy access later.

About This Project

Indiana Food Locator was developed as a minimum viable product to demonstrate how a centralized web application can improve access to information about food assistance resources. Future versions could include expanded geographic search capabilities, interactive maps, additional resource categories, and other community services.
